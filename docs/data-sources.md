# Data Sources & Provenance Registry

## Source Registry & Indicator Mapping

All metrics in the **Beyond GDP** dataset originate from verified multilateral repositories:

### 1. Macroeconomic Indicators
- **Indicator:** `GDP_bn` (Gross Domestic Product in Current Billion US$)
- **Agency:** World Bank National Accounts data, and OECD National Accounts data files.
- **Data Series Code:** `NY.GDP.MKTP.CD`
- **Citation:** World Bank Open Data (2023). Gross domestic product. Washington, D.C.

### 2. Demographic Statistics
- **Indicator:** `Pop_m` (Total Population in Millions)
- **Agency:** United Nations Population Division, Department of Economic and Social Affairs.
- **Series:** World Population Prospects: 2022 Revision / 2023 Country Estimates.
- **Citation:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs (2023).

### 3. Public Health & Longevity
- **Indicator:** `LifeExp` (Life Expectancy at Birth, Both Sexes combined)
- **Agency:** World Health Organization (WHO) Global Health Observatory (GHO).
- **Indicator:** `InfMort` (Infant Mortality Rate per 1,000 live births)
- **Agency:** United Nations Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation (UN IGME).
- **Indicator:** `Phys` (Medical Doctors per 1,000 Population)
- **Agency:** World Health Organization Global Health Workforce Statistics Database.

### 4. Labor Market & Education
- **Indicator:** `Unemp` (Total Unemployment as % of Total Labor Force, modeled ILO estimate)
- **Agency:** International Labour Organization (ILOSTAT Database).
- **Indicator:** `Tertiary` (Gross Enrollment Ratio in Tertiary Education, Both Sexes %)
- **Agency:** UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS) Education Database.

### 5. Environmental & Climate Metrics
- **Indicator:** `CO2_kt` (Carbon Dioxide Emissions from Fossil Fuel Combustion in Kilotonnes)
- **Agency:** Global Carbon Project / Emissions Database for Global Atmospheric Research (EDGAR).
- **Indicator:** `Forest` (Forest Area as % of Total Land Area)
- **Agency:** Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) Global Forest Resources Assessment.
